Juice and Water

~3 min5 screens

A classic puzzle about pouring back and forth.

Some juice goes into the water. Then the same amount of the mix goes back.
Is there more water in the juice, or more juice in the water?

Twice the water

This time you start with twice as much water as juice. The pouring is the same as before. Is there more water in the juice, or more juice in the water?

KEY IDEA

Some things don’t change

After you pour the same amount back, there’s always as much water in the juice glass as juice in the water glass. This holds however much you pour, even if the glasses start with different amounts or the liquids barely mix.

That one-for-one match is an invariant — something that stays true as other things change.
